EVs: Your Questions Answered

The below are a series of questions asked by participants during the 'State of the Electric Vehicle Industry: Market Status and Road Ahead' webinar, hosted by EE Business Intelligence and supported by The Electric Mission, Volvo Cars South Africa and everlectric, held on May 26, 2026.

Answers to the questions were collectively provided by webinar panellists Hiten Parmar, Executive Director at The Electric Mission; Ndia Magadagela, CEO at everlectric and Grant Locke, Managing Director at Volvo Car South Africa.

Can you expand on BEV registration growth amid the fuel crisis?

naamsa data showed an increase of about 50 percent to 7 000 vehicles in the year to date ending April 2026, compared with the same period in 2025. This figure covered plug-in hybrids, hybrids and battery electric vehicles. Further sales data is available from naamsa's Autolytics Bank.

Where does South Africa sit in the BRICS electric mobility evolution?

South Africa lags behind other BRICS nations in electric mobility development. India is highlighted as a strong example, with its shift to electric vehicles creating jobs and reducing oil import dependency. India has also placed emphasis on plug-in hybrid vehicles and biofuels.

Why are vehicle manufacturers less involved in installing public charging infrastructure in South Africa?

There is already some investment from manufacturers and private companies. Some Chinese manufacturers are involved in developing and installing charging infrastructure in South Africa in partnership with state-owned entities. More private investment is expected as demand becomes clearer. A related example is the Eskom/BYD partnership to accelerate South Africa’s electric vehicle future.

Are there projections for how EV growth may affect energy demand?

Some studies and reports have been completed. The short answer is that energy demand from electric vehicles is expected to be minimal over an overall fleet transition, especially when additional private generation and the Integrated Resource Plan are considered. The Energy Systems Research Group also incorporates EV transition into demand forecasts, including for the IRP 2025 demand forecast report.

Can electric vehicles be charged from a home solar PV system?

Yes. A low-power charger of about 2 kW can be used from a normal plug point, although charging will take longer. A wallbox typically allows up to 7 kW. For home PV charging, the outcome depends on the maximum output from the solar panels, the inverter and the timing of charging, especially whether charging takes place during peak sunlight hours.

What is the outlook for electrifying heavy-duty vehicles?

Battery electric medium-duty, heavy-duty and extra-heavy-duty vehicles from international manufacturers are already entering the local market. They are attractive for fleet and logistics operators, especially while fuel prices remain volatile. Moving freight from road to rail remains an ambition of the Green Transport Strategy, although rail infrastructure and reliability remain challenges.

Is there data on the contribution of ICE to EV conversions in South Africa?

Vehicle conversions are guided cautiously by national regulations, particularly compliance with homologation requirements from the original manufacturer.

What hydrogen refuelling facilities are available for fuel cell vehicles?

Hydrogen is described as unsuitable for mainstream transport use. It may have limited potential in specific niche applications, although it is viewed as impractical as a mass-market fuel.

What is the latest progress on EV range and charging times?

Charging speed depends on the voltage architecture of the vehicle. Vehicles using 800 V systems can support charging above 300 kW, although chargers with this capability are still limited in South Africa. Two charging stations capable of this have opened on the N3 through Zero Carbon Charge. Some of the latest EVs offer ranges of around 800 km on a single charge, and more of these models are expected to come to South Africa. Adding about 400 km of range can take around 20 minutes, depending on the starting charge level and charging curve.

What is the impact of needing to replace EV batteries?

Battery replacement is one of the most expensive aspects of an EV. The industry is already moving towards battery refurbishment, although this is still uncommon in South Africa. Many manufacturers offer battery warranties of eight to ten years. Evidence so far suggests that battery state of health is often better than expected. Accident damage remains one of the main battery replacement concerns. LFP batteries can typically support around 4 000 cycles, which can equal 800 000 km or more. This means there is a good chance that the battery will last for the life of the average vehicle.

How reliable are public chargers along the N3 between Johannesburg and Durban?

The response from personal experience was positive. With the introduction of Zero Carbon Charge stations, there is now more than enough charging capacity along the route.

Are EV batteries suitable for future grid support and second-life storage?

EV battery life is likely to be longer than the rest of the vehicle. Standardisation may be more important for charging systems, so that any charger can be used with any car. Older EV batteries can also have second-life applications in stationary storage, where size and weight matter less.

Does the total cost of ownership comparison apply to commercial fleets?

Yes. The total cost of ownership discussion focused on commercial fleets, although the same principles can also be applied when calculating ownership costs for passenger vehicles.

Is the EV community lobbying government for lower import duties and supportive policy?

Lobbying is continuing. Lower duties and supportive policy remain important areas of focus for the EV community.

What fuel and electricity prices were used in the rand per kilometre estimate?

One participant gave an example based on a Land Rover Discovery hybrid with a 20 kWh battery, which provides about 38 to 40 km of electric driving in town. The calculation can then be worked out from the battery size, electricity price and distance travelled.

How often do EV batteries need to be replaced?

Batteries are often guaranteed for thousands of cycles. Some webinar participants referred to 8 000 cycles or more, which could represent many years of use if charged daily. After many cycles, the battery does not simply stop working. Its storage capacity reduces over time. Such batteries can still be used in stationary applications where weight and space are less important. Vehicle manufacturers commonly offer battery warranties of eight years or more, and new-generation battery technology continues to improve.

What training programmes exist for young people entering the EV value chain?

The University of Johannesburg and Stellenbosch University offer relevant programmes through electrical and mechanical engineering. There are also private companies in South Africa offering short courses for qualified auto electricians and motor mechanics. ChrisnelSA and TÜV SÜD were mentioned. The Quality Council for Trades and Occupations has approved the National Transportation Electrician Qualification, and work is continuing on the national qualifications framework for workforce transformation. TVET colleges are also expected to move in this direction.

Can public charging help regional electricity distributors diversify their businesses?

Yes. Public charging gives electricity distributors an opportunity to diversify their business models and partner with the private sector to roll out infrastructure.

Can VWSA’s automated production line be adapted to produce EVs?

Yes. The referenced NAACAM study highlighted the flexibility of production lines to move from internal combustion vehicles to electric vehicles.

How is hydrogen developing as a competitor to EVs?

Hydrogen is generally viewed as expensive, energy intensive to produce and difficult to distribute. It is also described as less efficient than battery electric vehicles because green electricity must first be used to produce hydrogen, then the hydrogen must be transported, stored and converted back into electricity in the vehicle. Some participants felt hydrogen may eventually have niche uses, especially where excess renewable electricity can be converted into a high-density energy carrier.

Do European and UK compliance rules include recycled materials as well as emissions?

Yes. The regulatory focus has shifted from tailpipe emissions alone to the full life cycle impact of vehicles and batteries.

Why was the industry response poor for a tender to procure two EVs and a fast DC charging station?

The market is still developing. As EV adoption increases, vehicle availability and charger supplier participation are expected to improve.

Could Volvo search results be skewed because Volvo is moving away from ICE vehicles?

The broader response is that more market players are entering the EV space. As the market matures and adoption grows, both vehicle availability and charger participation are expected to improve.

What does a home solar charging system cost, especially if charging happens at night?

The cost depends on how often the vehicle needs to be charged. If the car can run through the week and only be charged on weekends, the required system may be smaller. For regular overnight charging from solar, home energy storage would be needed to balance supply and demand. The maximum capacity of the wallbox also limits charging speed.

What is the sentiment around EV fire risk and safety?

Fire risk remains one of the biggest public perception barriers to EV adoption globally. International data suggests that EV fires are generally less frequent than internal combustion vehicle fires. The industry response focuses on improved battery management systems, thermal monitoring, cell chemistry, vehicle crash protection and charging protocols.

Is there movement towards using EV batteries as part of home solar systems?

Yes. This was addressed through the discussion on bi-directional charging. Vehicle batteries can potentially support home energy use, although legislative and technical barriers still need to be addressed.

What is needed for motor retailers to establish accurate book values for EVs?

A proper used EV market needs to develop so that price discovery becomes more accurate. Original equipment manufacturers can help by providing evidence of residual value curves from global experience. Greater trust in battery life will also help. Battery state of health certificates could improve confidence in used EVs.

Which African country leads EV adoption?

Ethiopia was highlighted as a leader, with recent figures of 115 000 units in 2025 in its eMobility Strategy document. Kenya is also seeing rapid deployment of electric two-wheelers, and electric bus fleets are increasingly being deployed in East Africa.

What policy barriers are holding back wider EV adoption?

Policy barriers can be split into demand incentives and other enabling measures. Demand incentives could include lower company car tax or direct purchase incentives. Charging infrastructure and related market factors are also important. Policy helps adoption, although it is one part of the wider ecosystem.

What is behind the sharp rise in PHEV sales in South Africa in 2025?

The rise is likely driven partly by the introduction of lower-cost competitors. As prices fall, more consumers can afford plug-in hybrids. Battery electric vehicles are generally still more expensive than plug-in hybrids. Plug-in hybrids also reduce range anxiety for many consumers.

Why has EV adoption in South Africa been slow?

South Africa lacks supply-side measures that could stimulate the market. Such measures were described as low-cost or zero-cost options for government to implement.

Is V2G similar to SSEG?

Vehicle-to-grid means feeding electricity from the vehicle back into the grid. This would require permission or licensing to export electricity to the grid. Vehicle-to-load allows the car to power appliances directly, such as using an EV to run household equipment.

Are there battery banks in South Africa where EV batteries can be swapped instead of charged?

Battery swapping is mainly applicable to electric motorcycles. It is also being explored in some fleet operations in South Africa.

What is the outlook for EV resale and residual values after 10 to 15 years?

An EV can generally be owned for a long period. Once the used EV market becomes large enough, price discovery should improve. Confidence in battery life will be important. Battery state of health certificates can help build trust in used EVs.

Are Volkswagen and Stellantis making a mistake by focusing exports on ICE vehicles for the rest of Africa?

Some participants felt this would be a mistake, especially because many African markets consume used imports rather than new vehicles. The response also noted that South African manufacturers need to transform their manufacturing base towards zero-emission vehicles to remain aligned with key export markets such as the EU and UK.

Why is the Toyota EV still much more expensive than similar Chinese vehicles?

The Toyota bZ4X was described as being similarly priced to the BYD Sealion 7.

Can EVs be maintained outside the OEM dealership network?

Long-term support will be important. EVs have fewer regular maintenance items than ICE vehicles. Typical items include battery coolant, motor lubrication, tyres, suspension parts and brakes. Regenerative braking can reduce brake wear. Many components are also used across multiple models, which may improve local parts availability even if a specific EV model is discontinued.

What would attract OEMs to manufacture EVs in South Africa?

A clear commitment to decarbonising the economy and supportive policy would help. Proper incentives from the DTIC are also needed to improve EV affordability. Policy certainty, faster policy creation and sufficient market size are important. A 150 percent tax rebate for establishing an NEV production site has already been launched, although market size and export corridors remain key factors.

Is high-speed DC charging harmful to battery life over the long term?

Yes, it can have an effect, although the impact was described as small overall. Further reading was referenced from Geotab on EV battery health.

Is public charging priced the same as home charging?

Public charging is generally more expensive than home charging. One participant said public charging can be more than twice the price of grid electricity at home. Indicative figures mentioned were about R3.50 per kWh from Eskom and up to R8 per kWh for public charging.

How important is the Department of Transport’s Green Transport Strategy for accelerating non-ICE vehicles?

The Green Transport Strategy is important, although participants suggested that something stronger may be needed, such as an Act. The 2018 strategy has gaps, and there have been developments around policy revisions from the Department of Transport.
