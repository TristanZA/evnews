iCAUR 03T Electric SUV Arrives

Tristan Wiggill May 20, 2026 New Models

iCAUR South Africa has officially launched the 03T electric SUV, bringing a new contender to the country's expanding new-energy vehicle market. The model, positioned above the brand's existing V23 in the local lineup, goes on sale in South African showrooms from 28 May 2026 and is priced from R639,900.

The 03T is the latest offering from iCAUR, the new-energy sub-brand operating under the Chery Global umbrella. It arrives in two derivatives — a rear-wheel-drive 2WD and an all-wheel-drive AWD — giving buyers a choice between efficient single-motor performance and more capable dual-motor output.

The 2WD variant is powered by a single rear-mounted electric motor producing 135 kW and 220 N.m of torque, drawing from a 65.69 kWh battery. It accelerates from 0 to 100 km/h in 10.5 seconds and returns an NEDC-estimated range of 431 km at a consumption rate of 18.3 kWh/100 km.

The AWD derivative pairs a dual-motor setup for a combined output of 205 kW and 385 N.m, supported by a slightly larger 69.77 kWh battery. It covers the 0 to 100 km/h sprint in 6.5 seconds and offers an NEDC-estimated range of 436 km at 19.9 kWh/100 km. Both variants are limited to a top speed of 150 km/h and can be charged from 10% to 80% via DC fast charging in 30 minutes.

Bold Proportions and a Well-Appointed Cabin

Visually, the 03T adopts a squared-off, upright stance with pronounced wheel arches and slim LED lighting elements. Its all-aluminium multi-cavity cage body is engineered for corrosion resistance of up to 30 years, while reducing vehicle weight by more than 30% and lowering energy consumption by 3%.

A panoramic glass sunroof and 19-inch alloy wheels wrapped in 245/55 tyres complete the exterior. Five paint options are available, spanning Khaki White, Carbon Crystal Black, Cyan Grey, Starlight Silver, and Toronto Red.

Inside, the 03T seats five across a 2,715 mm wheelbase, with 450 litres of boot space that expands to 1,238 litres with the 60:40 split-folding rear seats lowered. A rear storage box adds a further 40 litres.

Both trims feature a 15.6-inch touchscreen, wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto, voice command functionality, a 540-degree surround-view camera, and a 50-watt wireless charger. The 2WD is fitted with an eight-speaker audio system, while the AWD upgrades to a 12-speaker Infinity setup.

Seat specification on the 2WD includes black leather upholstery, climate control, ambient lighting, and electrically adjustable heated and ventilated front seats with four-way lumbar support. AWD buyers gain a massage function for front occupants and an electrically adjustable passenger leg rest.

Safety and Ownership Support

The 03T carries six airbags across both trims, alongside a full suite of active driver assistance systems. These include Automatic Emergency Braking with vehicle, pedestrian and bicycle recognition, Adaptive Cruise Control, Lane Departure Warning and Prevention, Blind Spot Detection, and Traffic Jam Assist, among others. Front and rear parking sensors and ISOFIX anchorage points are standard throughout the range.

Shannon Gahagan, National Brand and Marketing Manager at iCAUR South Africa, described the 03T as a clear expression of the brand's identity. "Its design gives it a distinctive presence, but the real strength of the 03T is how it combines that personality with comfort, space, safety, and a choice of electric drivetrains suited to different customer needs," she said.

Both models are backed by an eight-year/200,000 km vehicle warranty, an eight-year/160,000 km power battery warranty for the first owner, a five-year/100,000 km service plan, and eight-year/200,000 km roadside assistance.

At R639,900 for the 2WD and R699,900 for the AWD, the 03T's pricing is particularly keen when compared to the plug-in hybrids from Jetour.
