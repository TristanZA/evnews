Hino adds hybrid trucks to Kinto

Tristan Wiggill May 19, 2026 Trucks

Hino South Africa has launched a limited batch of diesel-electric hybrid trucks locally, with the first vehicles already entering service through Namlog at the Toyota Africa Parts Centre in Ekurhuleni.

The company says it is initially importing 32 hybrid models in its 300 Series range, with eight already in the country. Three are currently in operation and a further five will join the fleet soon, while the remaining 24 units will be distributed to major fleet operators.

The trucks will be offered through Toyota’s international Kinto subscription model, which bundles the vehicle, servicing and maintenance into a single monthly payment. Hino says the system is designed to simplify fleet management, while Kinto Protect offers optional comprehensive insurance for fleet operators.

Hino says the 300 Series hybrids have been delivering fuel savings of between 15% and 30%, depending on the type of operation. At a time of high diesel prices and mounting pressure on transport operators to reduce emissions, that could make hybrids an increasingly attractive option for urban and regional delivery fleets.

The launch also highlights a pragmatic route to lower-emission freight transport in South Africa. While battery-electric trucks remain part of the global conversation, diesel-electric hybrids can cut fuel use and carbon emissions without requiring charging infrastructure or major operational changes.

Hino says the hybrid rollout forms part of its broader multi-pathway energy strategy. The company has also revealed that a prototype dual-fuel truck using diesel and hydrogen will be previewed in South Africa next month.
