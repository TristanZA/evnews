Geely Introduces E5 Apex Plus EV

Tristan Wiggill May 11, 2026 New Models

Geely Auto South Africa has expanded its electric vehicle (EV) line-up in South Africa with the introduction of the E5 Apex Plus, a midsize electric SUV priced from R788,888.

The new model forms part of a three-variant range that includes the E5 Aspire from R699,999 and the E5 Apex from R759,999.

The Apex Plus sits at the top of the range and is positioned as a longer range option for buyers seeking added convenience for daily use and extended travel. It competes against rivals like the (considerably cheaper) Dongfeng Nammi 06, among others.

Range and performance specifications

The E5 Apex Plus is powered by a front mounted electric motor delivering 160 kW and 320 Nm, enabling a 0 to 100 km per hour sprint in 7.6 seconds and a top speed of 175 km per hour.

Geely has paired this with a 68.39 kWh lithium iron phosphate battery, which provides a claimed WLTP driving range of up to 450 km.

Charging capability includes AC charging up to 11 kW and DC fast charging up to 100 kW. Under fast charging conditions, the battery can be replenished from 30 per cent to 80 per cent in about 20 minutes, supporting both urban commuting and longer distance travel.

The E5’s dimensions place it firmly in the midsize SUV segment, with a length of 4,615 mm and a wheelbase of 2,750 mm. Luggage capacity is rated at 461 litres, while the flat floor design associated with electric platforms contributes to cabin space and passenger comfort.

Inside, the Apex Plus features a 15.4 inch touchscreen infotainment system and a 10.2 inch digital driver display. Other standard equipment includes wireless smartphone charging, multiple USB ports, keyless entry, voice control and over the air software updates.

Leather appointed seats, power adjustable front seats and a panoramic sunroof underline its premium positioning.

Safety and ownership support

Safety features include adaptive cruise control, autonomous emergency braking, lane keeping assist, blind spot detection and rear cross traffic alert. A driver monitoring system and multiple airbags are also standard, alongside electronic stability control and hill descent control.

Geely has sought to strengthen the ownership proposition with a comprehensive aftersales package. This includes a six year or 150,000 km vehicle warranty, a six year or 120,000 km service plan and an eight year or 200,000 km battery warranty.

Buyers also receive a wall box charger, an emergency charging cable and a R12,000 charging voucher when financing through Geely Finance.

The company says the E5 Apex Plus is aimed at South African motorists looking to transition to electric mobility with lower running costs and reduced maintenance requirements.

With its combination of range, specification and pricing, the model represents Geely’s latest push into a rapidly developing local BEV market.
